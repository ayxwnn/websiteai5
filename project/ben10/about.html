Behind the Dial

About This Fan Build

A tribute to Ben 10’s classic era, curated to celebrate the aliens, narrative beats, and energy that made the original series iconic.

Project Blurb

“Ben 10: Classic Aliens Database” highlights the heroes unlocked in the original Omnitrix and showcases why each mattered to Ben’s journey. From Heatblast’s fiery first transformation to Ghostfreak’s haunting rebellion, the goal is to provide an accessible codex for fans and newcomers alike.
