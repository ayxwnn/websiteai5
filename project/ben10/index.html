Why the Omnitrix Matters

The Omnitrix is more than a watch. It stores DNA from countless species, letting Ben unlock entire civilizations’ experiences with a single transformation. Each alien expands his empathy, strategy, and responsibility.

Did you know? The original Omnitrix prototype included failsafes that limited transformation time, protecting host DNA from permanent rewriting.
